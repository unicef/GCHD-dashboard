# Translation glossary

Reviewers: keep these terms consistent across the catalog. Hazard and UN terms
follow UNTERM (https://unterm.un.org) where an entry exists.

## Never translated

- **Global Child Hazard Database** / **GCHD** — product name
- Dataset and asset identifiers (`NOAA/GFS0P25`, `projects/unicef-ccri/...`, band names)
- Model, product and source names: ECMWF SEAS5, NOAA GFS, GPM IMERG, ERA5-Land,
  MODIS, FIRMS, CAMS, Sentinel-5P, WorldPop, CHIRPS, ACLED, OISST, JRC, GIRI, FAO
- Index codes: ETCCDI R20mm, ET-SCI TXge35, BSS, AOD, MHC, MHI, PM2.5, PM10
- Season codes (OND, NDJ…) and units symbols (mm, °C, m/s, µg/m³, K)
- Country and region names (they come from the boundary data)

## Style

- Keep `{placeholders}` exactly as in the English; their order may change.
- Emphasis written in capitals in English (BOTH, NOT, THIS MODEL) is a deliberate
  caveat — keep an equivalent emphasis.
- The El Niño download disclaimer is legally sensitive: the English text is
  authoritative and is always shown alongside the translation.
